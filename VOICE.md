# Epic Wolf voice + copy laws (read before writing a single word)

Epic Wolf is a brand-new West Palm Beach agency (partners Jordan Gundlach and Shawn Wolf). PR, branding, digital marketing and business development, plus websites/software, and physical: signs, vehicle wraps, print and promo. Brand line: "Front page to front door." The wedge: no firm in Palm Beach County does the story (PR/brand), the build (web/software/digital) AND the street (signs/wraps/print) under one roof. PR firms don't print, sign shops don't do strategy, digital shops outsource both.

## Voice
- Confident, sharp, plain-spoken, a little edge. Short sentences. Talks like a senior strategist over coffee on Clematis, not a brochure.
- Specific over generic. Every paragraph should be impossible to paste onto another agency's site.
- Hyperlocal: use real places naturally (Clematis Street, CityPlace, Flagler Drive, Okeechobee Boulevard, Worth Avenue, Royal Poinciana Way, PGA Boulevard, Mizner Park, Atlantic Avenue in Delray, Downtown Jupiter / Harbourside, Wellington's equestrian season, I-95, Brightline). West Palm Beach is home; Palm Beach County is the territory.
- Audiences that are real here: finance and family offices ("Wall Street South"), real estate and development, hospitality and restaurants, healthcare and med spas, law firms, marine, equestrian, home services and trades, nonprofits and galas, retail on Worth Ave and Atlantic Ave.

## Hard laws (violations get rejected)
1. American English.
2. NO em dashes or en dashes anywhere. Use periods, commas, colons, parentheses.
3. No trailing ellipses.
4. Headlines (headline, h2, title fields) have NO commas and no em dashes. One clause. Compelling, not descriptive.
5. Never fabricate: no stats, client names, results, awards, years in business, team size, review counts, "#1", "best in", "award-winning", "guaranteed rankings", "trusted by 500 brands". No claimed relationships with specific journalists or outlets. You may name outlets as places a story could land ("the Palm Beach Post, the Business Journal, local TV") framed as targets, never as guaranteed placements.
6. Do not claim licenses, insurance, an owned print facility or owned install crews. Say "we handle it end to end" / "we manage production and install" rather than "our in-house print shop".
7. Do not name the airport (it was just renamed; avoid it). Do not mention SunFest (paused). Use "CityPlace" not "Rosemary Square".
8. Prices: only if you verified a widely published market range via web search, framed as "typical market range" with a source, never as Epic Wolf's price. Otherwise talk about what drives cost.
9. Never say "one person" or imply a one-man shop.
10. FAQ answers are answer-first: the first sentence directly answers the question in plain words (good for Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT). 40 to 90 words each.
11. No wolf puns more than once per page. No "unleash", "elevate", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "in today's digital landscape", "look no further", "take it to the next level".
